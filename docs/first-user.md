# First-user worksheet

Status: **not conducted**. No paying customer, time savings, realized profit, or real-world pricing accuracy is claimed.

Recruit one Australian used-laptop reseller who already sources ThinkPads. Observe rather than coach the session. Ask permission before recording identifying details or publishing quotes/screenshots.

1. What do you currently search, how often, and how long does screening take?
2. Pick a real sourcing task and record their usual method and task time.
3. Let them import or scan and inspect the shortlist. Record their time, useful suggestions, missed faults, and corrections.
4. Which evidence made a suggestion trustworthy? What was still missing?
5. Which listings would they actually investigate? Were availability, shipping, and actual condition checked?
6. Would they use it next week? What would make it worth paying for?
7. If appropriate, test an AUD 19/month support/update offer. Record whether they paid, expressed interest, or declined; interest is not payment.
8. What should change before their next session?

Record date, anonymized participant role, task, baseline/app time, suggestions investigated, errors, requested changes, and exact consented quotes. Track any eventual purchase and actual completed resale separately from estimates. Report no realized profit until a transaction outcome is known.
